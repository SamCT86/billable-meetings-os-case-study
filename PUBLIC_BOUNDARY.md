# Public disclosure boundary

This repository is a **public engineering case study**, not an open-source release and not a mirror of the private implementation repository.

## Intentionally public

- the agency/client billing problem;
- the agreement + evidence → decision model;
- the three-state public decision boundary;
- selected engineering trade-offs;
- defensible technical context;
- the AI-assisted workflow at a non-sensitive level;
- verification philosophy and current non-claims;
- the public product URL.

## Intentionally private

- application source code;
- internal prompts or agent instructions;
- exact schemas and rule-engine implementation;
- private reason-code internals and settlement mechanics;
- raw or customer-like meeting evidence;
- review tokens and private endpoints;
- credentials, secrets and infrastructure configuration;
- private repository paths, SHAs and internal document names;
- private commercial experiments and roadmap sequencing;
- implementation details that would materially reproduce the system.

## Why

The useful employer signal is the reasoning: **how I translate an operational agreement into explicit decision states, distinguish event data from contractual truth, preserve review when evidence is weak, and use AI without letting it invent the business rules.**

Public does not mean open source. No license to the private implementation is granted or implied by this case study.
